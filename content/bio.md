Hi! I am Jian Tang (Chinese: 唐健), a PhD student in the School of Computing Technologies at RMIT University, supervised by [Dr. Xiaoyu (Shaw) Xia](https://xiaoyushawxia.github.io/homepage/) and Dr. Jer Shyuan Ng.

Prior to this, I received my M.Eng. degree from the School of Big Data & Software Engineering at Chongqing University in June 2025 under the supervision of [Prof. Xiuhua Li](http://www.cse.cqu.edu.cn/info/2165/8493.htm), and my B.S. degree from Hunan University of Technology and Business in June 2022.

My research focuses broadly on **federated learning**, **edge intelligence**, and **system security**, with particular emphasis on privacy protection mechanisms, security coordination, and efficiency optimization. Building on my work in federated learning, my PhD research explores efficient and privacy-preserving fine-tuning of large language models in mobile edge computing networks.
Any form of collaboration is welcome; if you are interested in my research, please feel free to contact me via email.
