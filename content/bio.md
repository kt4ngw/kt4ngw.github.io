Hi! I am Jian Tang (Chinese: 唐健), a PhD student in the School of Computing Technologies at RMIT University, supervised by [Dr. Xiaoyu (Shaw) Xia](https://xiaoyushawxia.github.io/homepage/) and Dr. Jer Shyuan Ng.

My PhD research focuses on parameter-efficient fine-tuning of large language models (LLMs), with a particular emphasis on **low-rank adaptation (LoRA)**, across **heterogeneous and resource-constrained clients**. Although LoRA substantially reduces fine-tuning overhead by updating only a small set of additional low-rank parameters, clients such as smartphones, laptops, and edge servers differ considerably in computational capability, memory capacity, and communication bandwidth. My work aims to design efficient LoRA fine-tuning methods that accommodate such heterogeneity. I also investigate how to meet clients' privacy requirements, for example through differential privacy (DP), and how to safeguard LoRA fine-tuning against security threats.

Prior to my PhD, I received my M.Eng. degree from the School of Big Data & Software Engineering at Chongqing University in June 2025 under the supervision of [Prof. Xiuhua Li](http://www.cse.cqu.edu.cn/info/2165/8493.htm), and my B.S. degree from Hunan University of Technology and Business in June 2022.
